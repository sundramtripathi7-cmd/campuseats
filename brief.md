# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food service system. It helps students discover available food services, view food items, place orders, and manage their food orders.

## Who

The main users of CampusEats are:

* Students
* Food vendors
* Campus administrators

Students use the system to find food and place orders. Food vendors manage menus and prepare orders. Campus administrators manage the overall campus food service system.

## Nouns

The main nouns in the CampusEats system are:

* Student
* Vendor
* Administrator
* Restaurant
* Food
* Menu
* Food Item
* Order
* Payment
* Campus
* Delivery

## Verbs

The main verbs/actions in the CampusEats system are:

* Register
* Login
* Browse
* Search
* View
* Select
* Order
* Pay
* Cancel
* Prepare
* Deliver
* Track
* Manage

## Summary

CampusEats connects students with campus food services and provides the basic actions needed to discover food, place orders, process them, and manage the service.
